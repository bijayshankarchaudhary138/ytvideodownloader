Your YouTube video downloader — free, ad-free, no sign-up

Use this YouTube downloader to save any public video, Short, live replay, music track or playlist as a real file you can keep: MP4 in 8K, 4K, 1440p, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p or 240p, or audio-only as MP3 (up to 320 kbps), M4A, OPUS, WAV and FLAC. It works on Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, macOS and Linux — the whole app is a mobile-first web app, installable as a PWA, so there is nothing to download and nothing to install.

Why this downloader is different

  • Real 4K/1080p with audio. YouTube serves high resolutions as separate video and audio streams; we merge them with ffmpeg so you get one playable file — not a silent video.
  • Live progress. Real percentage, download speed and time remaining, streamed live — never a guessing spinner.
  • No ads, no popups, no fake buttons, no trackers, no sign-up.
  • Playlists and batches. Download a whole playlist (or several links at once) as a single ZIP archive.
  • Subtitles and thumbnails. Captions as .srt in any available language; thumbnails in every size up to 1280×720.
  • Trim before you download. Cut a clip (for example 00:30 → 01:15) without downloading the whole video.
  • Free documented API. The same engine is available as a REST + SSE API with an OpenAPI 3.0.3 document.
  • Self-hostable and open source (MIT). No company can shut this tool down or start charging you for it.

Link types that work

youtube.com/watch links, youtu.be short links, YouTube Shorts (/shorts/), YouTube Music (music.youtube.com), /embed/ links, live and premiered replays, and playlist links (?list=) — including the extra parameters YouTube adds (t=, si=, feature=, index=) and links copied from the mobile app's share sheet.

How to download a YouTube video (4 steps)

  1. Copy the link from YouTube — tap Share → Copy link on your phone, or copy the address bar on a computer.
  2. Paste it into the download box above and press Analyse.
  3. Pick the quality or format: 1080p MP4 for watching, MP3 320 kbps for music, subtitles, thumbnail, or a trim range.
  4. Press Download — watch the live progress, then save the finished file.

Frequently asked questions

Is this YouTube downloader free?

Yes — completely free, no account, no payment, no premium tier, no download limit badge. The code is MIT licensed and can be self-hosted, so it cannot be taken down or put behind a paywall.

Why do other sites only give me 360p or 720p?

Because YouTube serves 1080p and above as separate video-only and audio-only streams that must be merged with ffmpeg on the server. Sites without ffmpeg silently downgrade you to the 360p combined stream — or hand you a silent high-resolution file. Here the merge is mandatory and tested.

How do I download just the audio (MP3)?

Paste the link, open the Audio tab and choose MP3 320 kbps (or M4A, OPUS, WAV, FLAC). The audio stream is downloaded and converted with ffmpeg.

Can I download a whole playlist at once?

Yes. Paste the playlist link, press the playlist/batch button, and every item is queued and packed into one ZIP file with per-item status.

Can I download 4K or 8K video?

Yes, when the source has it. The format table lists exactly which heights are available for that video, with the real file size and codec, and 4K/8K are merged with their audio track like every other quality.

Does it work on Android, iPhone and iPad?

Yes — it runs in the browser on any device and can be installed as a PWA (Add to Home Screen) for an app-like experience with an offline shell.

Do you keep my files or track me?

No. There are no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking scripts. Files are stored temporarily and deleted automatically when they expire; the only thing the server counts is an anonymous per-IP rate limit.

Is downloading YouTube videos legal?

Download only videos you own, videos licensed for reuse (for example Creative Commons), or videos you have permission to download, and follow YouTube's Terms of Service and the copyright law in your country. This tool is for personal, permitted use.

Use it from the command line or your own app

Every feature on this page is available through a free REST API: create a job (POST /api/jobs), follow progress with server-sent events (GET /api/events) and download with HTTP range support (GET /api/files/{id}). The full OpenAPI 3.0.3 specification is served at /api/openapi.json — see the API documentation for copy-paste examples in curl, Python and JavaScript.
