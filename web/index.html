YouTube Video Downloader — download in 4K, MP3 and playlists

Paste a YouTube link and download the real file: 4K/1080p video with the audio track merged in, MP3 up to 320 kbps, entire playlists as a single ZIP, subtitles as .srt, every thumbnail size, and clip trimming. Free, with no ads, no popups, no fake download buttons and no sign-up.

Why this downloader is different

  • Real 4K with audio. YouTube serves high resolutions as separate video and audio streams; we merge them with ffmpeg so you get one playable file.
  • Live progress. Real percentage, download speed and time remaining — never a guessing spinner.
  • No ads, no popups, no trackers. Files are deleted automatically after they expire.
  • Playlists and batches. Download a whole playlist as one ZIP archive.
  • Subtitles and thumbnails. Grab captions in any language and thumbnails in every size.
  • Free API. The same engine is available as a documented REST API.

How to download a YouTube video

  1. Copy the video link from YouTube.
  2. Paste it into the download box and press Analyse.
  3. Choose the quality, format or subtitles you want.
  4. Press Download and save the file.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes — no account, no payment, no premium tier, and the code can be self-hosted.

Why do other sites only give me 360p or 720p?

Because YouTube serves 1080p and above as separate video and audio streams that must be merged with ffmpeg on the server. Sites without ffmpeg silently downgrade you to the 360p combined stream.

Can I download playlists?

Yes, and you get them as one ZIP file.

Can I get MP3 audio?

Yes, up to 320 kbps, plus M4A, OPUS, WAV and FLAC.

Do you keep my files?

No. Files are stored temporarily and deleted automatically when they expire.
